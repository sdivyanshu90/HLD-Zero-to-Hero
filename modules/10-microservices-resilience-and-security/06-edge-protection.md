# Edge Protection

WAFs and DDoS defenses protect internet-facing systems before application logic is overwhelmed.

## Web Application Firewall

A WAF filters malicious HTTP patterns such as injection attempts or suspicious payloads.

## DDoS Mitigation

DDoS defenses absorb, filter, or reroute massive traffic floods.

## Real-World Analogy

A WAF is a trained security screener. DDoS mitigation is a city-level traffic-control plan during a flood of cars.

## Trade-Offs

- Edge protection reduces attack pressure on the origin.
- Over-aggressive rules can block legitimate users.

## Interview Use

For public APIs, mention both application-layer and volumetric attack defense.
