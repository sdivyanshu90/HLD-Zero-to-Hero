# Byte Conversions

You need quick byte conversions because capacity estimates fail when units are sloppy.

## Essential Ladder

- 8 bits = 1 byte
- 1 KB is about 1,000 bytes in quick interview math
- 1 MB is about 1,000 KB
- 1 GB is about 1,000 MB
- 1 TB is about 1,000 GB

## Real-World Analogy

This is the number-system version of knowing meters, kilometers, and tons before planning freight logistics.

## Interview Use

Consistency matters more than exact binary-versus-decimal purity in fast interview estimation. State your assumption and stay consistent.
